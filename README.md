# FolioX

FolioX is one profile for your whole job search, no code needed. Fill it in once, or upload your resume, and you get a live portfolio at foliox.me/you, an ATS-friendly resume PDF, a daily list of jobs matched to your skills, AI resume tailoring for each application, and a tracker for every job you apply to. Developers, designers, students, and freelancers use it to keep their portfolio, resume, and applications in sync.
