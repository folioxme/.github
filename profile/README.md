## The problem

A job search usually lives in five places: a Word resume, a portfolio on another site, a spreadsheet
of applications, a dozen job board tabs, and a ChatGPT window for rewriting bullets. None of them
agree with each other, and keeping them in sync takes more time than applying.

FolioX keeps it all in one profile. When you edit it, your portfolio, your resume, and your
job matches all update from the same data.

## What you get

### Show your work

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Portfolio website** | Published at `foliox.me/you`. Mobile-first, light and dark themes, built-in SEO. No code. |
| **ATS resume** | Single-column PDF templates that recruiters and ATS parsers can read. Built from your profile, so it never goes stale. |

### Find the job

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Daily job matches** | A ranked shortlist from 15,000+ company career pages, scored against your skills, target roles, seniority, and location. Each job shows which skills you share and which you're missing. |
| **AI resume tailoring** | Pick a job and get your summary and bullets rewritten in the posting's language, plus a cover letter draft. Your main resume stays as it is. *(Pro)* |
| **Job tracker** | Follow each application from Saved to Applied, Interview, and Offer, with notes and the tailored resume attached. |
